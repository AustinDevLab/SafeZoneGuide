Stay in the circle.

A field guide to Ice Poseidon's Safe Zone event in Austin, Texas: stay in the safe zone for a chance to win $50,000, starting October 17.

  • 100 HP to start, and you carry 3 items that don't stack.
  • Leave the zone and after 5 s you lose 1 HP every 2 s.
  • Most actions need you within 5 m of the target.

Sigils

You pick one after the event starts and the choice is final. Tiers are my read of the rules, not tested in play.

Sigil game plans

Your sigil is final, so compare them here. Each plan covers the opening, midgame and endgame, then the best and worst outcomes.

Alliance planner

The game has no team system. Faith is the only real bond, and every other alliance is a handshake agreement that anyone can break. Choose how many of you are allying at the start to see which sigils fit together. Tap a sigil to open its game plan.

What each sigil does in an alliance

Alliance rules to know

  • Nothing protects an ally from you or you from them. Only a Faith bond has game rules behind it, and even that allows betrayal.
  • Faith bonds are strictly one-to-one, so Faith only works in pairs. A third Faith player has nobody to bond with.
  • Per the wiki, Necromancers are immune to every zombie, including each other's, so an all-Necromancer alliance can't be drained by its own swarm. An ally on any other sigil can be, so they must stay 5 m or more from your zombies, carry Zombie repellent, or be shielded.
  • Splash damage doesn't check loyalties. Your C4, claymores and unsafe circles hit allies too, and a dying Protection player damages everyone within 10 m.
  • Greed can steal from anyone within 5 m, allies included, and Death can rob any grave, including an ally's.
  • Only a Healing player can revive others, though anyone can revive a Healing player. They must be within 5 m of the grave inside 10 min. Missiles hit 25 m and nukes 100 m, but they land 3 min and 5 min after launch, so spread out when one is incoming.
  • A Death sigil player robbing a grave makes that player unrevivable, so never rob a teammate. Enemy Death players can do the same to yours.
  • Endurance players can't trade or receive items, but teammates can claim their care packages.
  • The site doesn't say how the prize works for a team. Agree on what happens in the final before the event starts.
  • Bigger than 5? Use pairs of Faith, one or two Healing players, and a looter or two. Avoid mixing Necromancy with non-Necromancers.

Builds

Twenty-seven builds, three for each sigil. You carry 3 items unless you pick Endurance, and Piety keeps all potions in one slot.

Endurance Top pick

Fortress

Endurance

Loadout: Hazmat suit, Body armor and Zombie repellent stay on you. Safety bubble, Shield and Guardian angel are your panic buttons, with Mirror forcefield and Anti poison for attackers.

Play: Claim every 2 h care package before anyone else. Spend Guardian angel on zone crossings and keep the Shield for nukes.

Weak to: You can't trade, and a Greed player within 5 m can steal your bubble.

Counter-puncher

Endurance

Loadout: Two or three Mirror forcefields, Anti poison, Shield, Hazmat suit and Body armor.

Play: Each mirror is used up by the next dart, drone, C4, claymore, knife, machete or fake chest, which goes back at the attacker. Ten slots let you hold several at once.

Weak to: Mirrors don't cover the zone, zombies, missiles, tear gas or nukes.

Drop ninja

Endurance

Loadout: Invisibility cloak, Smoke bomb, Hazmat suit, Body armor and Anti poison.

Play: Your 2 h drop is visible to everyone on the map. Smoke bomb blacks out contestants within 50 m for 20 min, and the cloak hides you for 1 h, so you can claim the package unseen.

Weak to: Players can still see you in person, and the cloak doesn't stop damage.

Necromancy Top pick

Gravekeeper

Necromancy

Loadout: Hazmat suit and Body armor, plus a Shield for nukes.

Play: Save your summons (3 per 6 h) until people are within reach. Every death gives you a zombie, and deaths within 50 m also add a guard zombie on the grave.

Weak to: Zombie repellent, Safety bubbles and anyone who walks away. Nothing here stops darts, drones or C4.

Fog of war

Necromancy

Loadout: Smoke bomb, Hazmat suit and Shield.

Play: A Smoke bomb blacks out everyone within 50 m for 20 min. They can't see zombies on the map, so yours close in unseen.

Weak to: One-use item, zombies are slow, and blacked-out players can still see you in person.

Zombie handler

Necromancy

Loadout: Drone, Poison dart and Hazmat suit.

Play: Your zombies pin people down. Use the Drone (10 HP after 30 s, any distance) and darts (within 5 m, 20 HP over 30 min) on the ones who walk away.

Weak to: Protection players and Mirror forcefields shrug off both items, and they're single use.

Piety Strong

Walking pharmacy

Piety

Loadout: A stack of up to 6 potions in one slot, then Hazmat suit and Body armor.

Play: Open every chest, since each is a potion. Heal after taking damage because potions can't be used at full HP. A Gigantic heals about 46 HP with your bonus.

Weak to: Chests only give potions, so armor has to come from care packages and trades. Fake chests still trap you.

Bunker

Piety

Loadout: Safety bubble, a potion stack and Hazmat suit.

Play: Drop the bubble when you're stranded or need a rest: 10 m, 2 h, immune to all damage and zombies. Top up with potions inside.

Weak to: It's stationary and visible to everyone, and anyone who steps inside is protected too.

Quartermaster

Piety

Loadout: A potion stack, Hazmat suit and Body armor.

Play: Open every chest and trade spare potions to allies (a trade holds up to 10 items, within 5 m). Bonded Faith partners can trade at any distance.

Weak to: Potions you trade take normal slots and lose your 15% bonus, and nothing binds an ally to you.

Greed Strong

Pickpocket

Greed

Loadout: Hazmat suit, with two slots kept open for what you loot or steal.

Play: Read inventories on the map and steal a Safety bubble, Shield or other best item from within 5 m (30 min cooldown). Your 2x chest reach adds a 25% chance of a free 20 min Shield per real chest.

Weak to: 90 HP, and getting within 5 m exposes you to darts, knives and claymores.

Blackout raider

Greed

Loadout: EMP, Drone and Hazmat suit.

Play: The EMP blinds every other contestant for 1 h: no map, no contestant details, no trading. You still see everyone, so line up Drone strikes (10 HP after 30 s) and walk up on a mark to steal.

Weak to: One legendary item, only 90 HP, and players can still see you in person.

Chest farmer

Greed

Loadout: Hazmat suit, Body armor and Bandage.

Play: Run a loop of chests with your 2x reach. Each real chest has a 25% chance of a free 20 min Shield and adds a blood token to your money bag.

Weak to: It depends on how many chests there are, and fake chests still cost you 5 HP.

Faith Situational

Bonded bastion

Faith

Loadout: Hazmat suit, Body armor and Bandage on each of you.

Play: Stay within 10 m so damage is halved and split, with each partner's own armor covering their half. Trade items at any distance.

Weak to: One death kills both, and either partner can betray the other.

Kingmaker

Faith

Loadout: Guardian angel, Bandage and Hazmat suit.

Play: Stay within 10 m so damage is halved and split. If your partner is stranded outside every safe zone while you're inside one, Sacrifice gives them all your HP and eliminates you.

Weak to: You're eliminated, and the bond is broken. Agree on the prize deal first.

Double-cross

Faith

Loadout: Body armor, Bandage and Hazmat suit.

Play: Bond for the half-damage, then betray from any distance when you're hurt and they're healthy. You take 30% of their current HP, and nothing can block it.

Weak to: The bond breaks forever for both of you, and your partner may be planning the same.

Death Situational

Vulture

Death

Loadout: Hazmat suit, Body armor and a Bandage.

Play: Save Death Ward for a zone crossing. Stay within 50 m of fights, since each death heals you 1 HP per contestant eliminated, and rob graves for 5% max HP.

Weak to: Quiet games give you no healing, and the heal caps at 60% max HP once 20% or fewer remain. Avoid graves with Necromancer guard zombies.

Border runner

Death

Loadout: Guardian angel, Hazmat suit and Shield.

Play: Chain Death Ward (first hit blocked, then 2 min of immunity) with Guardian angel (30 min) to cross open ground. Hazmat halves what's left.

Weak to: Death Ward has a 2 h cooldown, and the Shield is your only answer to a nuke.

Grave robber

Death

Loadout: Anti poison, Hazmat suit and Bandage.

Play: Rob every grave you reach for 5% max HP, which also makes that player unrevivable. Each death within 50 m heals you 1 HP per contestant eliminated so far.

Weak to: Fake gravestones look identical and poison you on a loot or rob attempt, and Necromancer guard zombies drain 2 HP per 5 s near graves.

Protection Situational

Ghost

Protection

Loadout: Invisibility cloak, Drone and Hazmat suit.

Play: For 1 h nobody can target you with darts, knives, machetes or drones, and you can still attack. Your Drone hits any visible contestant from any distance for 10 HP, and you're already immune to poison, missiles, C4 and claymores.

Weak to: The zone, nukes and a zombie already next to you still hurt, and potions heal you 50% less.

Demolitions

Protection

Loadout: C4, Claymore and Hazmat suit.

Play: Lay the visible Claymore on a chokepoint and the hidden C4 where people gather (20 HP within 10 m). You're immune to both as a Protection player.

Weak to: The wiki doesn't say whether that covers your own blasts. C4 is destroyed if you die, and your Retribution hits nearby allies.

Last stand

Protection

Loadout: Body armor, Bandage and Shield.

Play: Potions heal you 50% less, so lean on Bandages (10 HP over 2 min, lasting 1.1x as long) and armor. If you're cornered, Retribution hits everyone within 10 m for 10 HP when you die.

Weak to: The zone, nukes, tear gas, zombies and knives still hurt, and Retribution hits nearby allies too.

Healing Situational

Lifeline

Healing

Loadout: Bandage, Anti poison and Hazmat suit.

Play: Be the person others want nearby: heal 1 HP and cure poison every 30 min, and revive anyone up to 10 times. Stay within 5 m of an ally so you can be revived twice yourself.

Weak to: No strong tools of your own, and a Death player robbing your grave ends your revives.

Aid station

Healing

Loadout: Safety bubble, Bandage and Hazmat suit.

Play: Plant the bubble where your group needs a rest. Everyone inside is immune to all damage and zombies for 2 h, and you top people up.

Weak to: It's stationary and visible to everyone, and enemies who step inside are protected too.

Second chance

Healing

Loadout: Shield, Bandage and Hazmat suit.

Play: Any living contestant within 5 m of your grave can revive you up to 2 times inside 10 min, and you return with your leftover grave items. Pop the Shield once you're back.

Weak to: You return with only 5 HP, the reviver pays 5 HP, and a Death player robbing your grave ends it.

Trolling Just for fun

Trapper

Trolling

Loadout: Hazmat suit, Body armor and a Mirror forcefield.

Play: Seed up to 5 fake chests a day near real loot. Each claim costs the victim 5 HP and a 1 h chest lockout, and heals you 5. Save the unsafe circle for chokepoints and stay out of it.

Weak to: Mirror forcefields send fake chests back at you, and sigils show on the map unless anonymous mode is on.

Grave bait

Trolling

Loadout: Mirror forcefield, Bandage and Hazmat suit.

Play: Place your daily fake gravestone where fights happen. It copies a real grave, and every contestant who tries to loot or rob it, Death players included, is poisoned once for 20 HP over 30 min.

Weak to: Poison is slow, Protection and Anti poison stop it, and a Mirror forcefield sends it back.

Squeeze play

Trolling

Loadout: Guardian angel, Hazmat suit and Body armor.

Play: Put the 50 m unsafe circle over a camped spot (not on a person). Everyone inside loses 1 HP every 2 s with no grace period. Guardian angel and Hazmat let you hold the ground yourself.

Weak to: Shield, Safety bubble, Guardian angel and Death Ward block it, it lasts 20 min with a 2 h cooldown, and people can just walk out.

General game plan

Combat matters less than staying in the circle and keeping your HP up for a long event.

  1. PrepYour phone is the whole interface, and the nearby list needs live location. Bring a battery bank, good shoes and water. If you're teaming up, agree on sigils beforehand.
  2. Early gameDon't fight. Loot chests and care packages while everyone is spread out, and fill slots with always-on items first. Sigil, HP and inventory are visible on the map unless anonymous mode is on, so note who is Trolling, Greed or Necromancy.
  3. PositioningStay toward the middle of the zone, since it can shrink, speed up or split. Keep 5 m or more from anyone holding darts, knives or C4, and from Greed players. Drones work at any distance but give 30 s of warning.
  4. TrapsFake chests cost 5 HP and lock you out of chests for an hour. Fake gravestones poison you. Both look real, so be careful around anything a Trolling player just passed, and near graves with a Necromancer close by.
  5. Late gameSave the Safety bubble and Shield for the final collapse. No sigil is immune to nukes, but they land 5 min after launch, so you can walk out of the 100 m blast. Keep HP above about 25 and a Shield in reserve. Death and Necromancy players get stronger as people fall, so avoid fighting near them.
  6. AlliesA Healing player nearby is effectively a revive. Every alliance still ends at the finish.
